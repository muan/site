I feel like I may have talked about this before, but let me say this again~ When Effective Altruism was brought up to me by a friend in tech in a cafe in Brooklyn while we coworked, I asked him “who decides what is good and what is effective?” and that alone broke all the arguments for it. Unfortunately Peter Singer is too chummy with eugenics (see his controversy with disability rights activists) so I was skeptical from the get-go. 

Always ask people to define what is moral and what is good, just to make sure we are indeed on the same page.
