 > Just because this hasn’t hit you, I think _you should have a theory as to why you are favored by these platforms_. Then you should at least be worried that this would come (to you as well).

> Is it free when you suddenly spend hours wandering around random things you didn’t intend to? Is it free when you end up buying stuff you didn’t really want and wonder why you get it later? Is it free that when you feel that you’ve had your vulnerability exploited? I would say none of that is free. You are poorer in your own consciousness and in terms of attention and control of your life, and you’re poorer probably in misspent money.
