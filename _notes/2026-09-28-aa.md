The Drama (2026): I beg of you, please go to therapy. These people don’t know what introspection is and don’t know how to process their thoughts and emotions.

<details><summary>Spoilers</summary>

The reactions to Emma’s past from Charlie, Rachel, and Mike, are all terrible. These people seem to not know anyone who has ever been depressed. 

In my opinion a good reaction would be “Oh my gosh I am so glad you didn’t, I am so glad you got out of that phase and that I get to meet the you now, no longer burdened by the hate. Is there anything lingering? how can I support you?

People really do be classifying others as “monsters” as if we aren’t all.

This reminds me of the recent Netflix series on Ed Gein, which was massively editorialized, but it had quotes from Hitchcock about how people others think are monsters are really just people like them. And we need to stop being so surprised.

This also then reminds me of Foucault’s work around how behaviors get demonized then criminalized then pathologized. The gaze towards it could be more problematic than the behavior itself.
