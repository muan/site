Sent some friends to my therapist and heard back from them that my therapist misses me and wishes me well. She’s very glad that I have enough resources to support others and not just fighting for my own survival. I am proud of myself too.

The horror my friends have been put through by terrible men, oh boy, I can’t stomach the drama.

So a long awaited therapy note:

* Trust that I know best what’s best for myself
* Weight the needs clearly and know what I want to achieve so that I have grounds to stand on during the fight
* Keep trying but learn to expect failure (and recovery) so that the frustrations don’t hit as hard

A continuation of a theme this year, I am still annoyed by my own emotions, because they don’t seem justified. After much dissection, turned out there was a perfectly reasonable justification—I was frustrated that I could have trusted my instinct and cut ties early with a person, but I didn’t. I let myself be influenced.

Next time.
