Free State of Jones (2016): I liked it, but I like most war movies. This is better seen as a civil war film centering conflicts between white american southerners, while enslaved black people happen to be in it.

This got on my list because in Dimension 20 City Council of Darkness E11, Lou Wilson, a big McConaughhey fan, wouldn’t stop mentioning this film. It was very funny.
