I presume it is only a matter of time before Anthropic starts claiming that N% of the internet content is generated by Claude according to their secret algorithm, and that is the maximum extend I care with regards to AI watermark. The desecration of languages started at the inception of LLM, not now.

---

Perhaps an overlap to Gruber’s reaction (quoted), one continued frustration I have, is the fundamental ignorance towards _the wonders of languages_.

Written or spoken words both carry so much meaning and intention weaved into the context–from the interpersonal all the way to the zeitgeist–and they are being swept around like dust on a shelf.

The anthropomorphization, the butchery of _consciousness_, the naive classifying use of _intelligence_. Either the words have been hollowed out of their history and context, or it’s the _death of truth_ that has been plaguing every facet of our lives. Though, why not both?

WORDS HAVE MEANINGS.

I feel like an imposter making this point given my relationship with languages is almost purely practical and not literary. Nevertheless, I am too acutely aware of my own method and struggles with foreign languages (like now as I am writing this as [ESL/EFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_as_a_second_or_foreign_language)), to get on this bandwagon towards meaning dilution. And I feel a strong need to voice discontent in an attempt to stabilize my grasp of comprehension. 

It is not that literature is sacred, but that the destructive “evolution” of the primary method we use to converse and reach an understanding of different ideas, is not only under threat, but being dismantled. 

I feel so uncomfortably thinly tethered to reason.
