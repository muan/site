<details><summary>Obsession (2025) </summary>

[Also on Letterboxd](https://boxd.it/g59nmv).

It was…a captivating story. Seeing that most of my friends loved this film, I would usually not hesitate much and jump on the bandwagon. However, I actually felt so heavy throughout the film.

Considering, Bear did X accidentally, X made Nikki love him. He then did not consider if X was bad, if X hurt Nikki, or if it makes any sense at all that Nikki went from friend-zoning him to being madly and desperately wanting him. He accepted it and ran with it. And can we stomach this as a film if X = any day rape drug? 

I was very confused with how odd the “love” manifested by the wish was, but I guess, given Bear’s love is also contorted (see above), perhaps the wish simply replicated Bear’s version of “love” onto Nikki, where the loved one’s anatomy and wellbeing aren’t the priority at all. What matters is I get what I want, and therein lies the title of the film. I had just stated the obvious.

I don’t know. I found there to be so much that I want to sit with and unpack, not in the film but how I treat these words and feelings.

Other than that, great performance, the sound design be doing the most. 80% of the horror in the film was done through the sound IMO.
