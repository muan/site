I went to an art show with a friend who helped with the installation, and was introduced to the artist, whose work I have been interested in for a while. I know we have mutual friends and live close by, but never wanted to force a meet.

We are both socially reserved so the introduction was quite dry, until… “Wait, your name sounds familiar. Are you… the Mu-An?” I thought, uh oh, which reference are you going to think of. And she got super excited “Mu-An who bakes super delicious pretzels?!?! I am such a fan! I subscribe to your posts and have been too shy to message!” She said she feels like she already knows me so well.

I am so glad my hobby converted into this fast friendship. By the end of the show she was calling me “dear,” a welcome promotion. 

I guess this is how just being myself attracted friends my way, but I do still need to be outside to form these connections in real life.
