My impulse yearly Claude subscription ended last month. I initially used it to understand the history of AI (not LLM) and my interested Philosophical topics. Then when Claude Code was better (mid this year) I made a some personal stuff with it. At one point I was just hoping to make Anthropic’s finances worse by spending more than $20 worth of tokens per month. I don’t have other subscriptions to LLM services. 

Claude coded a German practicing app, a markdown writing and viewing app with a shortcut action, a Cloudflare Images and Stream iOS client, and lastly a one pager site for decorative web push notification (it’s on GitHub).

I’ve vibed and mentored the model for a few months. I don’t want to do this unpaid job anymore. Despite the built projects, it took much effort to (repeatedly) coax the model closely to make good enough things. Many of which I probably would have written properly on first take. I somewhat resent having done all this work but it’s still derived work from a machine that I feel repulsed to claim any credit.

As much as I enjoyed being able to use the word generating machines to generate words in ways I understand, it eventually breaks down and becomes really dumb. It made me form a hobby of seeking out university lectures, reading Wikipedia and research papers directly more and more. The increased reading material was also how I noticed that bimodal reading works much better for me.

Interestingly, these are mostly all in English. I rarely ever use LLM for topics in Chinese, perhaps because natively I feel more confident and find it easier to navigate and understand information “manually.”
