[The Revolt Against Technology (video)](https://youtu.be/vhYS1diXZMM)

“The Metrification of Human Lives” (around 23:30). The other day I dug into the algorithms used by EA to measure the efficacies, and they are really bad and insufficient–worth noting I am not suggesting there could be better algorithms, but that the idea that one could metrify sociological issues _on a global scale_ down to statistics, and then make meaningful changes based on those numbers, is a dangerously simplified concept that forsakes the complexity of human lives.
