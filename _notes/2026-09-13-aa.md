revisiting thoughts on American Psycho: it’s only satirical if one thinks what’s depicted is an exaggeration of society rather than the systemic and lived reality of people now and throughout history. and given it is not satirical but a depiction of a system and person working as the power that be intended, the use of artistically curated femicide is the selling point that caters to those who find entertainment in the display of cruelty. 

how does one find absurdity in American Psycho if they have already experienced versions of such absurdity in real life?
