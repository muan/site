> Women throw themselves into this man’s emotional blast radius, and he’s just so sad about it, but not sad enough to actually change how he treats them or himself—the misery is somehow the point, _unhappiness its reluctant reality principle_.

[A Roon with a View](https://www.bookforum.com/print/3102/a-roon-with-a-view-61275) - a book review of Intermezzo by Sally Rooney, by Brandon Taylor

I came across Brandon Taylor as a book reviewer, and I instinctively thought to check his review of Intermezzo, thinking it would be a quick way to judge if I would like his point of view. I do.

As a big fan of two of Rooney’s novels, I was very sad that Intermezzo got written in such an unreadable way. So I am glad to see Taylor pointing out the exhausting parts of the book. And indeed, I wish it was half a book, and while reading it I did _long for the return of sentences with subjects and verbs_.

Anyways, I thought that quoted paragraph aptly captured my impatience with those two men. Thankfully Beautiful World did not feature Nick’s internal monologue, or I would have been so put-off like I was with Intermezzo.
