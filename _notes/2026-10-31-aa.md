The Shining (1980): That’s a no for me. 

Firstly, the sound design was so funny. Images of the people playing flutes and hitting gongs appeared in my mind when scary things were supposed to be happening. That made me laugh out loud. 

Secondly, I found that I don’t find aggressive/violent people horrifying. I grew up with them. Sure I would want to avoid bodily harm, but not really fear. People who deceive and manipulate are way more horrifying than those who wear aggressiveness on their sleeve.

Thirdly, Jack Nicholson here is just very bland. I don’t think it’s very hard to play a douchebag. Shelly Duvall though? wonderful. She deserved better.
