My Chinese Borscht receipt:

- 1-1.5 part onions
- 1.5-2 part carrots
- 0.5 part potatoes (I just don’t like it much)
- 1 part beef ribs cubed
- 2 part tomatoes (some subbed by canned tomato dependent on fresh produce quality)

Salt and spices to taste.
I sometimes just put them all in no particular order then simmer in water, slow cook for hours, til carrots are fully cooked through. Other times I caramelize the onions then add tomatoes, then one by one each step with equal parts water, and lastly add poached cubed ribs to finish.

This is my comfort food ♥︎. Recently I’ve been adding aleppo peppers.

Growing up, each of the 5 members of my family had a specific favorite ingredient of the soup, so we always just had 1 part of each thing. As you see, making for one now, my preference is clear.
