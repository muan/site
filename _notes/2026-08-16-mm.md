[Hume Text Online](https://davidhume.org/) (davidhume.org) ought to be the top philosopher I have come across in recent years. So eloquent and so ahead of his time.

Edit: guess who then a week later read more works by him and came across multiple blatantly racist paragraphs. :/ Many argue that it's simply the period, everyone was like that somewhat. But who is "everyone"? and more to the point, I think _great thinkers_ is a title that should be reserved for people who very specifically are **not like everyone else**, is it not? This also reminds me how Bourdieu's construct of cultural capital relies heavily on 18 century classical music appreciation as a measurement, though it is also true that reach was way limited pre-internet and air travel.
