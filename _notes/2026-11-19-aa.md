The Killing (2012 season 2): She continues to be a bad parent. My friend said I have high standards, I disagree. My mom was neglectful as well and that wasn’t a fun memory. “You turned out well though” really isn’t something to say to someone who’s chronically suicidal. Being a responsible adult for a life you created is really such a low bar. 

I just want people to be honest about their limits and not try hard things *at other people’s expense*. By all means strive, become the best, chase your dreams, but other people are innocent, especially the ones who didn’t have a choice to become part of + dependent on your life.

Back to the main plot of the show (lol), I guess the twist was decent, and somewhat believable, can relate. I will keep watching.
